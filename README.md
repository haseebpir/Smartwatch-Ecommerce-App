## ✨ Overview

**WristMart** is a cross-platform smartwatch e-commerce app with a fast, beautiful shopping experience. Built with a frontend-first approach, it's designed to convert browsers into buyers with smooth navigation, rich product visuals, and an effortless checkout.

---

## 💼 Why Shoppers (and Clients) Love This

- ⌚ **Immersive Product Browsing** — High-quality visuals, 360° views, and detailed specs
- 🕶️ **AR Try-On Preview** — See how a smartwatch looks on the wrist before buying
- ❤️ **Wishlist & Favorites** — Save products to revisit and purchase later
- 🛒 **Smooth, Fast Checkout** — Minimal steps from cart to confirmed order
- 📦 **Live Order Tracking** — Real-time updates from purchase to delivery
- ⭐ **Reviews & Ratings** — Build buyer confidence with authentic customer feedback
- 🎯 **Personalized Recommendations** — Suggested products based on browsing behavior
- 💱 **Multi-Currency Support** — Ready for customers shopping from anywhere
- 🔔 **Deal & Restock Alerts** — Push notifications for price drops and new arrivals
- 🎨 **Buttery-Smooth UI** — Polished animations and transitions across the entire app

---

## 🧱 How It Works

| Feature | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Browse & Discover** | Users explore smartwatches with rich visuals and smart filters |
| **Preview Before Buying** | AR try-on lets users visualize the product on their wrist |
| **Add & Checkout** | A frictionless cart and checkout flow reduces drop-off |
| **Track the Order** | Real-time order status keeps customers informed after purchase |
| **Come Back for More** | Personalized deals and restock alerts drive repeat purchases |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Under the Hood)

**Mobile App:** React Native — single, high-performance codebase for iOS & Android
**Architecture:** Cross-platform frontend built for speed, smooth animations & scalability
**Integration Ready:** Built to connect with any e-commerce backend or payment gateway
**Focus:** Conversion-optimized UI/UX designed specifically for product-led shopping

This approach delivers a **fast, native-feeling shopping app** without maintaining separate iOS and Android codebases.
